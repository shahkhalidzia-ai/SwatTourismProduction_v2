Executive Feature Guide: What Tourists Can Perform

Tab 1: Home

Destination Discovery & Weather Alerts

Visual hero landing page with real-time weather warnings, road advisories, and curated destination showcases.

  • Active weather advisory alert banner at the top
  • Interactive valley cards (Kalam, Malam Jabba, Miandam)
  • Clicking valley opens rich Modal with forecast, photos & nearby hotels
  • Quick search bar to filter destinations across Swat
  • Verified stays and local attractions showcase
Tab 2: Explore

Verified Service Catalog & Room Selection

Unified marketplace for licensed hotels, certified 4x4 mountain jeeps, local guides, and authentic crafts.

  • Category filter chips (Hotels, Restaurants, Transport, Guides, Activities)
  • Area filter selector (Mingora, Kalam, Malam Jabba, Bahrain)
  • Hotel Detail Modal with room types and amenity lists
  • Room Selector with direct pricing (Deluxe, Presidential, Twin)
  • Government verification badge on every approved business
Tab 3: Trips (Itinerary)

Multi-Day Itinerary Builder & 4x4 Jeep Driver

Automated itinerary planner that organizes hotels, visits, and transport with continuous stay logic.

  • Multi-Day timeline (Day 1, Day 2, Day 3) with stay continuity badges
  • Assigned transport driver cards with direct Call and WhatsApp links
  • '+ Hotel' and '+ Transport' modals to add items to any specific day
  • 'Edit Trip' modal to update travellers counter, dates, and notes
  • Prominent 'Complete Trip' and 'Rate Hotel Stay' buttons on header
Tab 3: Bookings & Stays

Vouchers, Checkout & Hotel Ratings

Complete reservation management with instant digital vouchers, stay checkout, and verified star rating.

  • Reservation list with real-time status (Requested, Accepted, Confirmed)
  • Digital QR Voucher modal with printable check-in confirmation
  • 'Conclude Stay' button to complete hotel checkout
  • Gold 'Rate & Review' button opening 5-star rating dialog
  • Guest title and feedback comments sent directly to hotel manager
Tab 4: Safety

Highway Road Conditions & GPS SOS Distress

Real-time safety infrastructure providing road statuses and one-click distress dispatch.

  • Live road status cards (Kalam Road, Malam Jabba Pass, Bahrain Bypass)
  • Active meteorological authority weather warnings
  • Direct emergency contact numbers (Rescue 1122, Tourist Police, DHQ)
  • 'Trigger SOS Alert' modal with live simulated GPS coordinates
  • Distress alert transmits immediately to authority dispatchers
Tab 5: Profile

Tourist Preferences & Account Security

Personalization center tailoring the Swat experience to individual traveller preferences.

  • Budget level selection (Budget, Mid-range, Luxury)
  • Travel style selection (Solo, Couple, Family, Adventure group)
  • Interest tags (Trekking, Heritage, Food, Photography)
  • Emergency contact details (Name & Phone for mountain safety)
  • Multi-language support (English and Urdu)
