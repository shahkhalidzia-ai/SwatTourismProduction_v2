🗄️ Relational Database
PostgreSQL (Production) / SQLite aiosqlite (Development). 10 Module table clusters, indexing on listing types, areas, and foreign keys.
🔄 Alembic Versioning
10 Reversible migration versions enforcing complete schema consistency from initial accounts to profile fields and price item details.
📁 Blob & Media Storage
Local filesystem backend (pluggable to S3/Cloud). Handles verification PDFs, business licenses, listing galleries, and story images.
🌱 Seed & Reset Engine
Idempotent scripts (`seed_demo_data.py`, `reset_empty.py`) loading 8 areas, 4 destinations, 5 demo accounts, and 30 days of availability.
